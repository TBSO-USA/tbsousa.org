Upcoming Event

Kathina Robe Offering Ceremony

All Dhamma fellowships are warmly invited to join the Theravada Buddha Sasana Organization (TBSO) on 14th November 2026 (Saturday) for our annual Kathina Robe Offering Ceremony. Together we will offer lunch to the Venerable Sanghas, pay respect to the Triple Gem, offer the Kathina Robe and Kathina Tree, listen to Dhamma talks, and share merits with all beings. Seven monks will be in attendance. Lunch will be served to all visitors.

DATE 14th November 2026 (Saturday) TIME 11:00 AM - 3:00 PM (PST) PLACE 17730 Broadway Ave
Snohomish, WA 98296 TEL (360) 243-3468

Program Schedule

  • 11:00 AM: Offering Lunch to Venerable Sanghas
  • 11:30 AM – 12:45 PM: Serving Lunch to All Visitors
  • 1:00 PM – 1:15 PM: Paying Respect to the Triple Gem & Observing the Five Precepts
  • 1:15 PM – 1:45 PM: Offering Kathina Robe & Kathina Tree by Donors
  • 1:45 PM – 2:20 PM: Listening to Dhamma Talks
  • 2:20 PM – 3:00 PM: Sharing Merits to All Beings

Robe Donations

  • One complete Robe set: US$50
  • Upper Robe: US$30
  • Lower Robe: US$20

Main Sponsor

This year, in memory of Father U Than Nyunt, (Mother) Daw Khin Hlaing, (Daughter) Nway Nway + Ko Si Aung Family will be the main sponsor, donating the main Kathina Robe, the Kathina Tree, and fish soup to all visitors on that day.
